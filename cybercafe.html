Online Cyber Café Platform

A digital solution that simplifies access to online services like government forms, certificates, and document processing.

Project Overview

Online Cyber Café is a web-based platform designed to help users request essential online services without repeated physical visits. The platform focuses on clarity, accessibility, and trust.

The Problem

Users often struggle with complex government websites, unclear document requirements, and multiple visits to cyber cafés, leading to frustration and wasted time.

Project Goal

To design a simple and reliable platform that allows users to submit service requests online while maintaining transparency and ease of use.

Target Users

Students, job seekers, and individuals with low to medium digital literacy who require assistance with online services.

UX Process

The design process involved identifying user pain points, simplifying service categories, creating clear navigation flows, and validating usability through wireframes.

Wireframing

Low-fidelity wireframes were created to define layout structure, content hierarchy, and service request flows before moving to visual design.

User Flow

The user journey focuses on minimizing steps while ensuring clarity at each stage.

Impact

Metric Before After Platform
Service Requests Offline Only Online + Offline
User Visits Multiple Reduced
Status Transparency Low High

UI Design

The UI emphasizes simplicity with clear service cards, readable typography, and minimal visual clutter to support accessibility.

Final Outcome

The platform improves accessibility to essential digital services while reducing dependency on physical cyber cafés and increasing user trust.
